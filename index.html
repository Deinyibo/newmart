Your money is held until you're happy

When you pay online — by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack — NewMart holds your payment in escrow. The store prepares and ships your order, and only when you tap Confirm delivery is the money released to the seller, minus our 5% commission.

Something wrong? Raise a dispute and our team reviews it: we refund you or release to the seller. No confirmation, no release.

Prefer pay on delivery? It's built in.

Choose Pay on delivery at checkout. We verify your phone number with a one-time code, then the order moves: Preparing → Shipped → Delivered → Cash collected.

New buyers have a ₦50,000 pay-on-delivery cap until their first successful orders. Sellers receive the 5% commission as an invoice — no card needed to sell.
